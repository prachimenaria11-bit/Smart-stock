SmartStock - Neighbourhood Market Intelligence
===============================================
Run: unzip, then double-click index.html (works offline, no install, no server).

Demo login: demo@smartstock.in / demo1234

Features: login/sign up, light+dark theme, English/Hindi, voice alerts (browser speech),
store guide with route, inventory (add/edit/restock/export CSV), smart alerts,
product sliders, offer pop-ups, Hana chatbot (rule-based).

Notes:
- Data and accounts are saved in the browser (localStorage) only - demo, not production security.
- Sales chart uses sample numbers.
- Hindi voice needs a Hindi voice installed on the device (Chrome/Edge recommended).
